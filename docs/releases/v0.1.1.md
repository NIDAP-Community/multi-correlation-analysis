## Included behavior

- Default circle/bubble correlation heatmap: area encodes correlation magnitude and color encodes direction.
- Optional `--heatmap-style square` colored-square correlation heatmap.
- Category-specific correlation, raw p-value, and Benjamini-Hochberg FDR matrices.
- Optional L2P gene-symbol normalization and the documented duplicate-gene policies.
